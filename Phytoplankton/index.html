Monitoring Environmental Factors Influencing Phytoplankton Variability

Google Earth Engine · Remote Sensing · Sentinel-2 · MODIS-Aqua

Google Earth Engine Remote Sensing Sentinel-2 MODIS Time Series Geovisualization

Project Objective (วัตถุประสงค์)

Utilized Google Earth Engine (GEE) to analyze and monitor environmental variables affecting phytoplankton changes over time using remote-sensing data. The analysis tracks Chlorophyll-a (proxy for phytoplankton biomass) alongside its physical drivers — Sea Surface Temperature, PAR, wind, and precipitation — over the Upper Gulf of Thailand at 4.6 km, and inside the Bangkok province inland waters at 10 m using Sentinel-2 NDCI. โปรเจกต์ใช้ GEE วิเคราะห์ปัจจัยแวดล้อมที่ส่งผลต่อความผันแปรของ แพลงก์ตอนพืชจากภาพถ่ายดาวเทียม ครอบคลุมทั้งระดับอ่าวไทยและในแม่น้ำ/คลอง กรุงเทพมหานคร

Methodology Workflow

Filter by AOI & date │ Monthly compositing (mean / sum) │ ├─► Period-mean maps (climatology) │ ├─► Time-series charts (AOI spatial mean per month) │ ├─► Seasonality (month-of-year) │ ├─► Anomaly map (latest month − climatology) │ └─► Per-pixel Pearson correlation per driver

How to Run

  1. Sign up for a free Google Earth Engine account at earthengine.google.com/signup.
  2. Open the GEE Code Editor.
  3. Pick a tab above, click Copy code, then paste into a new GEE script. Save & Run.
  4. To publish as a web app: Apps → New App → Public. Copy the resulting URL and paste it into the box above to embed it on this page.
  5. To export results, open the Tasks tab in GEE and run the CSV / GeoTIFF exports.

Resume / Portfolio One-liner

Utilized Google Earth Engine (GEE) to analyze and monitor environmental variables (SST, PAR, wind, precipitation) affecting phytoplankton (Chl-a) variability over the Upper Gulf of Thailand and Bangkok inland waters, involving multi-sensor remote-sensing data fusion (MODIS-Aqua, Sentinel-2, ERA5, CHIRPS), monthly compositing, time-series analysis, and per-pixel Pearson correlation mapping.
